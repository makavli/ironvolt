# IRONVOLT: the story

## The Long Dusk

In 2079 the Voltline, the continental grid that fed every city from the coast to the salt flats, failed in a
single night. Nobody agrees on why. What everyone remembers is the fire: forty reactor towers burning at once,
and an ash cloud so thick that the sun has never cleared the horizon since. Seventeen years later the sky
still sits at dusk, all day long. People call it the Long Dusk, and the children born under it have never seen a
blue sky.

Power is the only currency left. Whoever holds a working tower decides who gets light, water pumps and heat.

## The Rust Legion

The Rust Legion started as salvage crews stripping the dead grid for copper. Under **Warlord Ottar Graive** it
became an army. Graive found the one tower that still turns, **the Cradle**, at the centre of the old grid, and
built his empire on it: towns that kneel get power; towns that do not stay dark. To keep the Cradle running the
Legion needs people who understand it, so it takes them: grid technicians, linemen, engineers, dragged from their
towns in chains.

The Legion wears what it scavenges: olive armour cut from old utility trucks, gas masks against the ash, red lenses
so they can see through it. Its grenadiers wear rust-red plate. Its drones are rebuilt survey drones from the old
grid company. Its war machines are mining crawlers fitted with guns.

## The Ironvolt Brigade

The Brigade is what is left of the Voltline's own repair crews: the people who used to climb the towers. They kept
their insulated suits, their visors and their tools, and learned to fight. They are few. They move fast, they carry
what they find, and they free every technician they reach, because each one freed is one less pair of hands
keeping Graive's grid alive, and one more person who knows how to turn the lights back on.

- **Kestrel "Kes" Adeyemi** (player 1, ember armour, cyan visor): a former tower climber, the fastest on a ladder
  in the whole grid. Her brother was taken in the first Legion raid.
- **Juno Varga** (player 2, blue armour, gold visor): a lineman's daughter who rebuilt her first rifle from a
  transformer housing. She talks to machines and swears at them in the same breath.

## Mission 1: Scrapyard Dusk

The Ashfield scrapyard is the outer ring of Legion ground: a plain of wrecked trucks, dead pylons and cooling
towers at the edge of the old grid. The Legion holds five captured technicians here on their way to the Cradle.
Free them, push through, and break the gate.

The gate is **BASTION**, a deep-mining crawler from the grid's old quarries. The Legion bolted a mortar to its back,
a gun to its chin, and a missile rack that fires blind on spotted coordinates. Its glowing core is a salvaged grid
capacitor; when the armour cracks, the core shows, and it pulses faster the closer it gets to failing.

## Mission 2: The Prisoner Line

The technicians freed at Ashfield were the lucky ones. The rest had already been moved: the Legion ships its
prisoners to the Cradle by rail, along the last line of the old grid's own railway, over a viaduct that crosses a
drowned town. The ash is thicker here, the dusk colder, and the only lights left are the line's red signals.

The train is pulled by **WARDEN**, an armoured engine the Legion built from a grid maintenance locomotive. Its roof
carries a turret, its cow-catcher is welded steel, and when its whistle blows it runs down anything on the track.
Behind it, the prisoner cars.

The Brigade stops the train and breaks the cars open. Kes searches every one. Her brother is not aboard, but the
prisoners know his name: he was taken off the line weeks ago and sent ahead, straight to the Cradle, because he
knew how to read the old grid's maps.

## Mission 3: Pylon Road

The last line of the grid that still carries power runs from the Cradle out across the salt flats: forty kilometres
of high-tension towers over a cracked white plain. Its service road is the only way in. Out here the ash is thinner;
for the first time in years the dusk has colour in it, and the sun is almost a disc.

The Legion guards the line with **sappers**, demolition troopers in hazard yellow who carry no rifle, only a satchel
charge, and with **HARROW**, the grid company's own line-survey gunship. Before the fall it flew the towers checking
insulators; now it carries Legion plate, a chin gun and a bomb bay, and it flies the line looking for anyone on the
road.

The Brigade brings it down on the road. In its flight log Juno finds survey runs flown to a pattern: the gunship has
been mapping the dead lines, tower by tower, from maps drawn by a prisoner. Kes knows the handwriting. Graive is not
just holding the Cradle. He is relighting the old grid, one line at a time, and every line he relights he owns.

## Threads for later missions

- The Cradle is not just a power plant: something in it kept running on the night the grid burned. Why only that one?
- Kes's brother was sent ahead to the Cradle, for the grid maps he can read. What does Graive want mapped?
- Graive wears a technician's badge from before the fall. He was one of them.
- The ash is thinning near the Cradle. Somebody there is trying to bring the sun back, or keep it away.
